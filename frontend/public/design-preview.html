The Protocol

Four steps. Zero intermediaries.

The oracle value never touches the blockchain. A mathematical proof is all the verification required.

I
Lock Collateral
Deposit USDC into the Soroban smart contract. Funds are locked until the oracle condition triggers or the policy expires — fully automated, zero intermediary required.
II
Condition Triggers
When the oracle price crosses your threshold — BTC below $50,000, ETH above $3,000 — the policy becomes claimable. The market is your arbiter. The contract your enforcer.
III
Generate ZK Proof
A Groth16 proof is generated locally in your browser. The oracle value is committed via Poseidon hash — it never leaves your machine. Only the proof travels on-chain.
IV
Collect Payout
The Soroban contract verifies the proof via native BN254 host functions. USDC transfers to the beneficiary in the same transaction. No approval. No delay. No dispute.
Why ClaimProof

Insurance without trust.

No adjusters, no paperwork, no human discretion. The contract settles or it doesn't.

Zero-Knowledge Privacy
The exact oracle price at claim time never appears on-chain. A Groth16 proof certifies the condition was met without revealing a single byte of the private oracle reading.
Fully Trustless
No human can override a valid proof, delay a payout, or deny a legitimate claim. The Soroban contract is immutable. The mathematics are incontestable by design.
Instant Settlement
Verification and payout occur in a single Stellar transaction. The moment a valid proof is confirmed, the collateral is released to the beneficiary. No waiting period of any kind.
